# Changelog

## 1.0.8
- Songs that Pandora sends without cover art now get one looked up by artist and title (Apple's public iTunes Search; only the artist and title are sent). The station's art is used only if no cover is found.
- Cover images try more sizes of Pandora's own image (1080, 500, 300 and 130 pixels) before falling back.
- The notification and lock screen use the song's own art before the station's.

## 1.0.7
- Text size now moves in 5% steps (80–200%), with a separate interface size (70–160%) and a Reset sizes button.
- Tapping the playback notification opens the app on the Playing tab.

## 1.0.6
- Fixed the status bar in the White theme.
- Replay now walks back through earlier songs, one per press.

## 1.0.5
- Added a Replay button that plays the last played song.

## 1.0.4
- Fixed unreadable black text on the Black and dark Color themes.

## 1.0.3
- Added a setting for which tab Android Auto opens first (For You or My Stations).
- Station art in the car is cropped to a square.

## 1.0.2
- Android Auto browse screen and controls reshaped after the official app; "My Collection" renamed "My Stations".

## 1.0.1
- Android Auto controls match the official app and show the station name.
- Added in-app updates from GitHub releases.

## 1.0.0
- First release: stations with pinning, search and A–Z sort; station options; now playing with History and Up next; likes and dislikes; Color, White and Black themes with e-ink paging; audio quality, sleep timer, battery and background playback controls; resume of the last station; Quit; Android Auto.
